## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business analysis of a simulated dental clinic based on 12 months of operational data. The objective was to identify the key factors affecting profitability, evaluate doctor performance, analyze client retention patterns, and formulate strategic recommendations to improve overall business outcomes.

The analysis was conducted entirely in Google Sheets, demonstrating how spreadsheet tools can support data-driven decision-making and business optimization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goals of this project were to:

- Rebuild the clinic's financial reporting model using the REPORT sheet;
- Formulate and test analytical hypotheses related to profitability;
- Evaluate doctor performance from both financial and customer perspectives;
- Analyze client behavior and retention patterns;
- Develop a strategic proposal for Q5 aimed at maximizing clinic profitability.

---

## 🔬 Analytical Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1
Clinic unprofitability is associated with doctor inefficiency, excessive workload, and staff shortages.

### Hypothesis 2
Clinic unprofitability is associated with the quality of doctor service and its impact on patient retention.

---

## 📊 Analysis Blocks

### Block 1 – Appointment Request Dynamics

- Analysis of MISSED versus SCHEDULED appointment requests;
- Identification of demand bottlenecks;
- Evaluation of doctor workload distribution;
- Assessment of staffing capacity.

### Block 2 – Service Quality and Retention

- Doctor performance analysis using CQFB, CSFB, CPFB, and FB indicators;
- PS analysis;
- Investigation of the relationship between customer satisfaction and profitability;
- Identification of factors influencing repeat visits.

### Block 3 – Client Audience Analysis

- Client age segmentation;
- Visit frequency analysis;
- Retention analysis by doctor;
- Evaluation of long-term customer value.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Approximately 20% of all appointment requests (900 out of 4,497) were missed due to insufficient doctor capacity.
- Zoya Ushakova achieved the highest client retention rate (59.20%), largely driven by patient courtesy and relationship quality.
- Pyotr Aybolit generated the highest profit (€199,878), primarily due to strong professional performance.
- Higher levels of professionalism appeared to inversely correlate with perceived service quality across doctors.
- Staffing constraints had a measurable impact on clinic revenue potential.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions were proposed:

- Optimize workforce allocation to reduce missed appointment opportunities;
- Increase staffing capacity during peak demand periods;
- Share best practices among doctors with strong retention performance;
- Balance operational efficiency with service quality initiatives;
- Focus on retention strategies to maximize long-term profitability.
